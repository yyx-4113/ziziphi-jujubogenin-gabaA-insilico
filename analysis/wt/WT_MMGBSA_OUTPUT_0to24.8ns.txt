Window dependence (a reportable limitation)
-------------------------------------------
The same wild-type trajectory, evaluated over different 100-frame windows, gives

    0-24.8 ns : dTOTAL = -38.06 +- 2.14 kcal/mol   (this artifact)
    30-50  ns : dTOTAL = -32.40 +- 1.73 kcal/mol   (pipeline log only)

a 5.66 kcal/mol spread. Because the wild-type reference enters every ddG in the
alanine scan as an additive constant, this spread shifts all six ddG together and
leaves their ranking and signs unchanged; it nevertheless bounds how quantitatively
any single ddG may be read.
